# ROBYLO — Business, Website and Design Concept

Status: working notes from November 16, 2026. These are project intentions, not validated performance claims or an approved visual design.

## Purpose and audience
ROBYLO helps small and growing US businesses, initially in the Chicago market, establish a strong digital foundation, attract customers and develop over time. Position it as a direct, experienced digital systems partner for business owners, not as a large agency or a cheap template website seller.

## The homepage: three things, nothing more complicated
A business owner should immediately understand:
1. **I understand your problem.** You need customers and revenue; weak websites and disconnected digital presence can hold you back.
2. **I know how to help.** We can build or improve the website, visibility, customer inquiries and practical digital workflows.
3. **Here is proof.** Show real projects and verified results.

Write in plain, natural American English. No technology stack, AI jargon, marketing buzzwords or long lists on the homepage. Do not promise guaranteed customers, sales or rankings. Use a concise hero, an understandable solution, prominent proof and a simple contact action. Details belong on service and case-study pages.

## Services to develop
- Websites: design, development and improvement
- Local SEO / Google Business Profile
- Google Ads and digital marketing
- Practical AI integrations and business automation
- Ongoing digital systems and website support

Package and price these later. Sell outcomes, not technologies.

## Portfolio
Aim for 5–6 real, permission-cleared cases. Chicago Mounting is the primary case-study candidate: a growing service business with an ongoing website, SEO and digital partnership. Document actual baseline, work performed, dates, evidence and measurable outcomes before making public claims. Other potential cases previously discussed include GCPRO Restoration, ColoPro Properties, Glenview Smiles, an HVAC site and Elite Mechanical Services; verify rights, contributions and client consent before publishing.

Case presentation: elegant diagrams, lines, circles, capability indicators and visuals, but no invented scores or results. Show the problem, solution and evidence.

## Visual direction
Strict monochrome: white, off-white, light/medium/dark gray, near-black and black. Medium-to-dark gray body text must remain readable against white; strong headlines and emphasis near black. Premium editorial/Swiss-inspired typography, intentional whitespace, asymmetric compositions, distinctive but usable buttons, restrained motion and accessible focus/hover. Avoid repetitive generic SaaS card grids and excessive decoration.

Request three distinct visual homepage concepts:
1. Editorial Minimalism
2. Modern Experimental
3. Architectural Monochrome

Each should show a full desktop homepage and, if supported, a mobile adaptation. The owner may provide additional website and visual references later. Select a direction before implementation. This is visual exploration only, not authorization to change WordPress.

## Technical implementation plan
- Start with the homepage and a presentable minimum site: homepage, services, portfolio/case studies and contact.
- Existing WordPress + Elementor; inspect actual installed environment and MCP access before changes.
- Reuse lessons and proven technical patterns from GCPRO where appropriate, but do not copy its brand, proprietary materials or unreviewed code.
- Respect this repository's existing AGENTS.md, AI-CONTEXT.md and Elementor Atomic V4 responsibility split.
- Prefer native Elementor settings/components, minimal child-theme code, accessible responsive implementation and actual browser validation.
- Keep website content and business concept distinct from reusable starter infrastructure.

## Business approach
Solo founder supported by AI tools; no immediate need to hire. Seek a manageable group of ongoing small-business clients with defined monthly retainers and separately scoped projects. Chicago Mounting provides a practical relationship model, but private payment details are not for public case studies. Initially prioritize a credible website and direct, personalized outreach over paid advertising. Do not rush to leave existing employment; legal permission to operate, immigration/employment constraints and business registration require independent verification.

## Permanent visual quality standard — owner direction, 2026-11-18
Homepage V2 remains the structural baseline. The owner's Apple references establish precision, compact proportions, distinct typography roles and controlled whitespace, not Apple branding. Mobile must not require excessive scrolling because of oversized type or gaps. Preserve Manrope, monochrome, semantic headings, gray secondary lines and the 1200px desktop grid.

Use the existing Atomic classes and components as the source of truth. Inspect consumers before changing shared styles; do not duplicate a typography system, scatter overrides or rebuild working interactions. Current homepage hierarchy: display 76/56/36px, section H2 44/36/30px at desktop/tablet/mobile (700); service and Approach H3 24/22/20px (600). Portfolio mobile titles remain owner-selected 20px and descriptions/actions 12px. Supporting copy uses role-specific 14–16px mobile sizes, not a universal tiny font size.

Keep text groups together, consistent gutters, restrained image-caption gaps, usable touch targets and real keyboard focus. Verify actual rendering at 1440/1024/768/390/320px, including full pointer release/leave, keyboard and touch behavior. Settings alone are not proof. Compare native trees before/after as well as screenshots. Preserve native list semantics. Record known limitations rather than masking them with blur handlers or extra JavaScript.
